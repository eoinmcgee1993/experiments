Virtual work is tempting to any manager hoping to save money, reduce employees’ commuting and travel burdens, and increase efficiency.

Although so many people expect to work 100% remotely, I'd like to warn you. It can be extremely costly for your business! 💵

Let me quote one of the studies: "Our results indicate that at least for projects of short duration, virtual teams yield significantly lower performance, lower satisfaction, and a lower results to effort ratio".

It seems to me that certain aspects of communication, such as a coffee conversation, a conversation overheard by accident or laughing in the same room, cannot be easily replaced.

And what about full body language, which plays such an important role in communication? Can we simply skip it, especially when forming a team? 🤔 

That may not be a popular view, but I'm a great supporter of the #hybrid work model. I think it's the best compromise between work #flexibility and keeping high #performance, #motivation, team #coherence and high-quality #communication. 

For complex products these are all must-haves.

Do you have a different opinion? I would love to hear about your experiences! 😀
