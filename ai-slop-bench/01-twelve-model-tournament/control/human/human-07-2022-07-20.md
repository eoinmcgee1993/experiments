Leading others is a PM superpower. 

8 ways to 10x your leadership skills:

✅ 1. Stay humble

Learn to feel good about not being the smartest person in the room. Listen "actively" with awareness, processing what's being said rather than who's saying it.

"Greatness is to stay humble when the whole world calls you great!" — Mehmet M. Ildan

✅ 2. Show your vulnerabilities

Do not pretend to be someone you are not. Being authentic is an essential part of developing meaningful relationships. People can genuinely connect with and feel close to others when they expose their vulnerabilities.

"In order for connection to happen, we have to allow ourselves to be seen, really seen." — Brené Brown

✅ 3. Respect everyone

Respect everyone. Judge people by their results and decide based on what's right, not whom you like.

Be thoughtful of others' feelings, address mistakes with kindness (everyone makes them!), and treat people equally (including the cleaner).

"Respect comes in two unchangeable steps: giving it and receiving it."— Edmond Mbiaka

✅ 4. Lead without authority

Instead of giving precise instructions, co-create goals and let your teams find the right path. Great leaders are excellent communicators, able to clearly and concisely explain their goals and persuade others.

"The authority is largely irrelevant — if you are a leader, you will lead when you are needed." — Clay Scroggins

✅ 5. Stay open-minded

Get out of your comfort zone. Be widely open to testing new ideas. Welcome arguments and information that does not match what you already know.

"Progress is impossible without change, and those who cannot change their minds cannot change anything." — George B. Shaw

✅ 6. Keep calm

Though emotions call for immediate actions, you can only act rationally and make better decisions by not letting them take control.

Great leaders stay peaceful, no matter the circumstances. In crises, they use data and operate on facts. It's as if they can "stop time," see the bigger picture and then make the next move.

"You don't have to control your thoughts. You just have to stop letting them control you" — Dan Millman

✅ 7. Act like a gardener 

Invest in growing and developing people. Realize that the best investment you can make is in others.

"Gardeners don’t try to command plants to grow with a detailed Work Breakdown Structure in a Gantt chart and milestones. With care, they tend to the garden, planting seeds, watering them, and when done, producing a varied and flourishing garden."—Jonathan Smart

✅ 8. Decide and take full responsibility

Although some decisions require more time, great leaders never delay them longer than necessary.

Don't hesitate to make the final decisions and take full responsibility for your actions.

“Chronic indecision is not only inefficient and counterproductive, but it is deeply corrosive to morale.“— Robert Iger
