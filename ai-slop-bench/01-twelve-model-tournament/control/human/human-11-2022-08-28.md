Do you want to break into PM?

Here are the top 5 red flags in job offers:
 
🚩 1. Multitasking
🚩 2. Resources
🚩 3. Project / schedule
🚩 4. Customer proxy
🚩 5. SAFe
 
Now in detail:
 
 🚩 1. Multitasking
 
Multitasking is a myth. 

According to the research presented by Gerald M. Weinberg, each additional project or task is a loss of ~20% of productivity. When we add a third project, context switching consumes almost 50% of the effort.
 
Great PMs focus on what's the most important and delegate or ignore everything else.
 
🚩 2. Resources
 
How is it possible that in the 21st century, people are still so commonly referred to as “resources”?
 
People will not work the same way, being moved from place to place or working overtime. They are not tools that you can use however you like. They also have dreams and ambitions.
 
People are not resources. They are skilled professionals. 
 
🚩 3. Project / schedule
 
A typical project mindset involves:
- Assigning tasks to team members
- Pushing instead of letting others pull the work
- Emphasis on predefined outputs (features) and "scope"
- Affection for detailed schedules
- Constantly asking about the team's progress
- Pursuing the myth of 100% utilization (thanks Gawain Lee-Son)
 
Great product managers collaborate, inspire, show their trust, and understand that working on a product is complex. They embrace the unknown and encourage experimentation.
 
🚩 4. Customer proxy
 
Let me tell you about the product death cycle trap. 

It happens when the product manager forgets about the "Why" and becomes a waiter. There is no vision or strategy. To please everyone, PM collects and waterfalls all the requirements to the team. 

Although customers know where they are in pain, they cannot design a solution. Those features do not solve anyone's problems and do not drive the expected business results. 

The most harmful solution is to ask customers again what features they need. The cycle repeats itself.
 
🚩 5. SAFe
 
Every time I see SAFe's diagrams, something dies inside me. This heavy, fake "agile" framework enables bureaucratic, top-down control, disempowers teams, and promises false predictability.

SAFe is one of the best ways to kill agility, and limit your growth as a PM.

-

If you see any of those red flags, think twice. You may avoid serious harm to your growth and career.
