7 Chrome extensions that boost productivity so much they feel illegal to use.

All free:

1. Bionic Reading®  - Guides your eyes by highlighting the initial letters of each word. A fantastic way to read longer texts (e.g., blog posts). I use it primarily with Medium.

2. StayFocusd - Blocks time-wasting websites. I have repeatedly found that eliminating distractions is essential to focus on deep work (a flow state).

3. Glasp  - Allows you to take notes and add highlights on web pages. Everyone should know it. It delivers what it promises.

4. ScribeHow - Allows you to record the screen and immediately convert any procedure (e.g., a sequence of steps in the app) into a tutorial. A great way to share knowledge in the organization.

5. Calendly - Eliminate the hassle of back-and-forth emails to find the perfect meeting time—an excellent, free alternative to Microsoft Bookings.

6. Grammarly - Correct spelling, grammar, punctuation, clarity, etc., as you write. You can use the free version, although it is worth paying for. After a few months, I can't imagine working without it.

7. Todoist - A great way to organize & monitor tasks and plan your days. The feature I love is adding sites as tasks, e.g., LinkedIn posts, tweets, Substack articles, etc. It definitely speeds up my learning.

I do not use: 
- AI writing assistants
- AI paraphrases
- Content schedulers

So I have not included any of them.
