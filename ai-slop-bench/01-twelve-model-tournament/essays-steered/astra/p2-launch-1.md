You can now get a daily summary of your open support tickets right in Slack.

Our new Slack integration posts the summary to a channel you choose, giving your team a simple way to keep track of outstanding support work without switching tools. Send it to your support channel to keep the team in sync, or choose a shared channel where other teams can stay informed.

It’s a quick way to see what’s still open and start a conversation when something needs attention. The tickets stay in your helpdesk, while the daily summary brings them into a place your team already checks.

To get started, connect Slack to your helpdesk and choose where you’d like the summary to appear. We’d love to hear how it fits into your team’s day.
