You can now get a daily summary of your open support tickets right in Slack.

Our new Slack integration posts the summary to a channel of your choice, so your team can keep an eye on outstanding requests alongside the conversations they’re already having. It’s a simple way to make open support work more visible without someone having to pull together and share an update each day.

Send it to your support channel to keep the team aligned, or choose a shared channel where other teams can stay informed. Your helpdesk remains the place to manage tickets. Slack gives everyone a daily reminder of what’s still open.
