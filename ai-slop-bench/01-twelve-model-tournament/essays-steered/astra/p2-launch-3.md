You can now get a daily summary of your open support tickets right in Slack.

Our new Slack integration posts the summary to a channel of your choice, giving your team a regular check-in on what’s still open without having to leave the conversation.

Send it to your support channel to keep the team in the loop, or choose a shared channel where colleagues across the business can follow along. It’s a simple way to make outstanding support work more visible and give everyone a starting point for discussing what needs attention.

Slack won’t replace your helpdesk, but it can make it easier to stay connected to the work happening there. We’re excited to bring that daily view to a place your team already uses.
