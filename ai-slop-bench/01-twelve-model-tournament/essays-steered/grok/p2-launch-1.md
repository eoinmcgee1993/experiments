Hey team. We just shipped a Slack integration a bunch of you have asked for over the past few months. Once a day it posts a short summary of your open support tickets into whatever channel you choose. That’s it. No extra dashboards, no new place to check. Just a quiet recap so the people who live in Slack can see what’s still waiting without opening the helpdesk first thing.

You pick the channel. You pick the time that makes sense for your team. The post itself is simple: how many tickets are still open, a quick split by priority if you use it, a handful of the oldest ones so nothing sits too long, and a link back if someone wants to jump in. We kept it light on purpose. The goal is to meet you where you already are, not to turn Slack into another ticket queue.

A lot of you told us tickets get lost in the shuffle when half the company never leaves chat. This doesn’t replace the helpdesk. It just gives the rest of the team a daily pulse so fewer things fall through. If your morning standup already happens in Slack, now the open-ticket picture is sitting right there.

To turn it on, go into settings, connect Slack, choose the channel, and you’re done. It takes a couple of minutes. We’re starting with the daily summary and we’ll listen to how you actually use it before we pile on more.

Thanks for sticking with us. Let us know how it feels once it’s running.
