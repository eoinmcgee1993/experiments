We just launched something a lot of you have been asking for. You can now hook your helpdesk up to Slack so a daily summary of open support tickets posts itself into a channel you choose.

Most of the teams we talk to already live in Slack. Switching over just to see what is still waiting was becoming a small daily tax. This puts the recap right where people already are. The post covers how many tickets remain open, a short look at the ones that have been sitting longest, and who they are assigned to. You pick the channel and roughly when it should arrive. Changing the settings or turning it off later takes almost no time.

It lives in the usual spot. Open Settings, go to Integrations, connect Slack, pick your channel, and you are done. A couple of minutes at most. It is available right now for every existing customer on every plan, no extra charge.

Give it a try when you have a spare minute and let us know how it feels after a week. We built it because you asked, and we want it to actually save you a few clicks each morning.
