Hey everyone, we just shipped a Slack integration we think a lot of you will actually use. It posts a daily summary of your open support tickets to a Slack channel you choose, so the queue shows up where your team already spends the morning instead of living only inside the helpdesk.

We kept hearing the same thing: tickets get missed when people are hopping between tools, and the first fifteen minutes of the day disappear into a status recap. This is meant to cut that down. Once a day, a short recap lands in Slack with what is still open. Your team can scan it, assign work, and jump back into the tickets without a separate login just to see the list.

You pick the channel, so it can sit in your support room, a leadership channel, or wherever that visibility actually helps. Setup is in your account settings. Connect Slack, choose the channel, and you are done. You can change the channel or turn it off whenever you want.

If something looks off while you connect it, reply here or reach us the usual way. We would like to know how it lands with your team.

Thanks for sticking with us. Hope this saves you a few minutes every morning.
