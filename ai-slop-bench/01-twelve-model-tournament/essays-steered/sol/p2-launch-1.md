We’re excited to introduce our new Slack integration, designed to help your team stay on top of open support tickets without leaving Slack.

Once connected, the integration posts a daily summary of open tickets to the Slack channel of your choice. Your team gets a clear view of what still needs attention, making it easier to prioritize work, spot growing queues, and keep everyone aligned.

You can choose the channel where summaries appear and adjust the setup at any time. Send updates to a dedicated support channel, a team workspace, or wherever your team already coordinates its day.

To get started, open your helpdesk settings, select Integrations, and connect your Slack workspace. Choose a channel for the daily summary, confirm your settings, and you’re ready to go.

The Slack integration is available now. We hope it makes your daily support workflow simpler and helps your team resolve tickets faster.
