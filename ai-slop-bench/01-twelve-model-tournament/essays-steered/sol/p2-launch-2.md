We’re excited to introduce our new Slack integration, designed to help your team stay on top of open support tickets without leaving Slack.

Once connected, the integration posts a daily summary of open tickets to the Slack channel of your choice. Your team gets a clear, shared view of what still needs attention, making it easier to spot outstanding issues, coordinate next steps, and keep customer requests moving.

The summary brings ticket visibility into the place where your team already communicates. Whether you send it to a dedicated support channel or a broader customer operations channel, everyone can start the day with the same picture of the current queue.

You can set up the integration from your helpdesk settings by connecting your Slack workspace and selecting a destination channel.

We hope this makes managing your support workload a little simpler and helps your team respond with greater clarity and consistency. Connect Slack today and put your daily ticket summary where your team will see it.
