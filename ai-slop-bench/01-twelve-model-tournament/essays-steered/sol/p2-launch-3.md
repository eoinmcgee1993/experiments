We’re excited to launch our new Slack integration, designed to help your team stay on top of open support tickets without leaving Slack.

Once connected, the integration posts a daily summary of open tickets to the Slack channel of your choice. Your team gets a clear view of what still needs attention, making it easier to prioritize requests, spot potential delays, and keep everyone aligned.

You can choose the channel where summaries appear and make sure the right people have visibility into the support queue each day. Whether you use a dedicated support channel or share updates with a wider customer team, the daily summary keeps important work visible without adding another manual task.

The Slack integration is available now. Visit your helpdesk settings to connect your workspace, select a channel, and start receiving daily ticket summaries.
