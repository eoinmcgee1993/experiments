## Your open support tickets, delivered daily in Slack

Keeping your team informed about outstanding support requests just got easier. Our new Slack integration posts a daily summary of your open support tickets to the channel you choose.

Whether your team coordinates in a dedicated support channel or a shared customer operations space, you can bring ticket visibility into the conversation—without manually compiling and sharing a daily update.

**With the new integration, you can:**

- **Stay informed:** Get a daily snapshot of open support tickets.
- **Keep the right people in the loop:** Send summaries to the Slack channel that works best for your team.
- **Reduce manual updates:** Spend less time sharing ticket status and more time helping customers.

Your helpdesk remains the place to manage tickets. Slack gives your team a convenient way to stay aware of what’s still open.

**Ready to get started?** Set up the Slack integration, choose your channel, and make your daily support summary part of your team’s routine.
