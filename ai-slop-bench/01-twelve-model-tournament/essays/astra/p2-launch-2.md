## Your open support tickets, now in Slack

Keeping track of open support tickets shouldn’t mean asking for another status update.

Our new Slack integration brings a daily summary of your open support tickets straight to a channel you choose—so your team can stay informed where they already work.

**With the Slack integration, you can:**
- **Keep open tickets visible.** Give your team a daily snapshot of support work that’s still in progress.
- **Share updates in the right place.** Send your summary to the Slack channel that makes sense for your team.
- **Cut down on manual check-ins.** Bring a regular update into the conversation without having to write one yourself.

Whether you review support requests in a dedicated channel or keep the wider team in the loop, the daily summary gives everyone a shared starting point for follow-up.

**Get started by connecting Slack and choosing a channel for your daily summary.**
