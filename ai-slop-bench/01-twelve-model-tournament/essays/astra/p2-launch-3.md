## Your open support tickets, summarized daily in Slack

Keeping an eye on your support queue just got easier. Our new Slack integration posts a daily summary of your open support tickets to a channel you choose—so your team can stay informed where they already work.

Whether you share updates in a dedicated support channel or a broader team channel, you can give the right people a regular view of what’s still open without manually putting together a daily recap.

**With daily summaries in Slack, you can:**
- **Keep open tickets visible.** Bring a daily snapshot of your support queue into your team’s conversations.
- **Cut down on manual updates.** Spend less time compiling and sharing ticket summaries.
- **Keep everyone on the same page.** Give your team a shared starting point for discussing outstanding support work.

Slack keeps your team informed; your helpdesk remains the place to manage and resolve tickets.
