**Your daily ticket summary, now in Slack**

Starting today, you can have a summary of your open support tickets delivered to Slack every morning — no dashboards to open, no reports to run.

**What it does**

Connect your workspace, pick a channel, choose a time, and each day you'll get a single post that shows where your support queue stands. The summary includes:

- Total open tickets, with the change since yesterday
- Breakdown by priority and status
- Tickets that have been waiting longest for a reply
- Anything approaching or past its SLA target
- Tickets assigned to each agent, so the team can see the spread at a glance

Every ticket in the summary links directly back to the conversation, so anyone in the channel can click through and pick it up.

**Why we built it**

Most of you told us the same thing: the queue is fine when someone is watching it, and the trouble starts when nobody is. Tickets slip when the person who usually checks is out, when a busy week pushes support to the background, or when the people who *could* help — engineering, account management, leadership — simply never see what's piling up.

Putting the summary where your team already spends the day changes that. It's a nudge, not a notification storm. One post, once a day, in the channel you choose.

**How to set it up**

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations → Slack** and click **Connect**.
2. Authorize the connection with your Slack workspace.
3. Choose the channel where the summary should post.
4. Set the delivery time and time zone.
5. Save. Your first summary arrives the next morning.

You can filter the summary by team, queue, or tag, and you can set up more than one — for example, a full-queue summary in `#support` and a high-priority-only summary in `#leadership`. Anyone with admin access can configure it.

Note that this is a read-only integration. It posts summaries but does not create, edit, or close tickets from Slack, and it won't send anything other than the scheduled summary.
