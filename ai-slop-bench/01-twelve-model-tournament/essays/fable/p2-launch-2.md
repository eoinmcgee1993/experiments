**New: Daily Ticket Digest for Slack**

Starting today, you can have a summary of your open support tickets delivered to Slack every morning — no dashboard required.

**What it is**

The Daily Ticket Digest posts one message per day to a Slack channel you choose. It gives your team a shared, at-a-glance view of where support stands before the day starts, so nobody has to ask "what's still open?" in a standup or dig through their inbox to find out.

Each digest includes:

- Total open tickets, with the change since yesterday
- Breakdown by priority and status (new, waiting on customer, waiting on us)
- Tickets approaching or past their SLA, listed individually with a direct link
- Oldest unresolved ticket and how long it's been open
- Tickets assigned to nobody

Every ticket mentioned in the digest links straight back to the helpdesk, so you can act on it in one click.

**Setting it up**

If you already have Slack connected, this takes about a minute:

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations → Slack**
2. Click **Add Daily Digest**
3. Pick a channel, a delivery time, and the views you want included
4. Save

If you haven't connected Slack yet, you'll be prompted to authorize the app first. Only admins can create or edit digests; anyone in the channel can read them.

**Making it yours**

You can run more than one digest. Many teams set up a company-wide summary in a general channel and a more detailed one for the support team. Each digest can be filtered by team, queue, tag, or assignee, and scheduled independently — so your EMEA and US teams can each get their own at the right local time.

Prefer weekdays only? Toggle off weekends. Want it quiet when there's nothing to report? Turn on "Skip if no open tickets."

**A few things to know**

- The digest is read-only. It won't create, close, or reassign tickets from Slack.
- It respects your existing permissions. If a queue is restricted, only digests configured by users with access to it can include its tickets.
- Delivery times are in your account's timezone, and you can override this per digest.
- It's available on all plans at no additional cost.

**Why we built it**

The most common request we heard this year was some version of "I just want to know what's open without logging in." The dashboard is great when you're actively working tickets; it's less great for the manager checking in before a meeting or the engineer who wants to know if their fix cleared the backlog. Putting the summary where people already are — Slack — felt like the right answer.
