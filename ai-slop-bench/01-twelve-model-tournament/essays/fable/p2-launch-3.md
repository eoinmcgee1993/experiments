**Your open tickets, delivered to Slack every morning**

Starting today, you can have a daily summary of your open support tickets posted directly to a Slack channel of your choice. No dashboards to open, no reports to run—just a clear picture of where your queue stands, waiting for you when you start your day.

**Why we built this**

Many of you told us the same thing: your team lives in Slack, and the hardest part of managing support isn't handling tickets, it's remembering to check on them. Escalations slip. Aging tickets go unnoticed until a customer follows up. A manager has to ask "what's still open?" more often than anyone would like.

The daily summary puts that information where your team already is, so the whole group starts the day with the same view.

**What's in the summary**

Each post includes:

- Total open tickets, with the change since yesterday
- A breakdown by priority and status
- Tickets that have breached or are approaching SLA
- The oldest unresolved tickets, with direct links
- Tickets assigned to no one

Every ticket referenced in the summary links back to the full conversation in your helpdesk, so you can jump straight from Slack to action.

**Make it yours**

You choose the channel, the time it posts, and the time zone. Want separate summaries for different teams? Set up multiple summaries, each filtered by team, queue, tag, or assignee. A regional support lead can get a summary for their region; an engineering liaison can get only tickets tagged "bug."

You can also toggle individual sections on or off. If SLA tracking isn't relevant to a particular channel, leave it out.

**How to set it up**

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations → Slack**
2. Connect your Slack workspace (if you haven't already)
3. Click **Add daily summary**, pick a channel, and set your schedule
4. Choose your filters and sections, then save

The first summary will post at the next scheduled time. You can send a test post immediately to check the formatting.

Admin permissions are required to connect a workspace; any user with reporting access can create or edit summaries once the connection is in place.

**Availability**

The Slack daily summary is included on all plans at no additional cost, and it's live in your account now. If you already use our Slack integration for real-time ticket notifications, the daily summary works alongside it—you don't need to reconnect anything.
