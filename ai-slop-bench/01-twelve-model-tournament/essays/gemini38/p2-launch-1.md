**Subject: New Feature: Keep your entire team aligned with Daily Ticket Digests in Slack**

Hey team,

Support doesn’t happen in a silo—yet keeping everyone aligned on the ticket queue often requires endless status meetings, dashboard screenshots, or asking non-agents to log into the helpdesk just to see what’s pending.

Starting today, you can bridge that gap effortlessly. We’re excited to introduce our new **Slack Integration: Daily Support Digests**. 

Now, you can automatically send a high-level summary of your open tickets directly to the Slack channel of your choice, every single morning.

---

### What’s Included in the Daily Digest?

Delivered at a time that works for your team, your automated digest gives everyone an immediate pulse on customer support health, including:

* **Queue Snapshot:** Total open, pending, and unassigned tickets.
* **Priority Flags:** A clear callout for high-priority or SLA-breaching issues that need immediate eyes.
* **Direct Links:** One-click access to jump straight from Slack into the filtered queue in your helpdesk dashboard.

### Why You’ll Love It:

* **Better Cross-Functional Visibility:** Drop the digest into `#product-feedback` or `#leadership` so stakeholders stay informed on support volume without needing a helpdesk seat.
* **Focused Standups:** Start your support team’s day aligned around the exact backlog numbers in `#support-internal`.
* **Zero Disruption:** Get the insights you need directly where your team already collaborates.

---

### How to Set It Up in 3 Minutes

Setting up your digest is quick and requires no complex configuration:

1. Head to **Settings > Integrations** in your dashboard and select **Slack**.
2. Authenticate your workspace and select **Add Daily Digest**.
3. Choose the target channel (public or private) and set your preferred delivery time and timezone.

That’s it! Your chosen channel will receive its first automated digest the next business day.
