**Subject: New Feature: Keep your entire team aligned with daily Slack digests 🚀**

Hi team,

Keeping everyone updated on customer issues shouldn’t require endless status meetings or manual exporting. More importantly, critical tickets shouldn’t slip through the cracks just because someone isn’t logged into the helpdesk all day.

That’s why we’re excited to introduce our newest feature: **Daily Support Summaries for Slack.**

Starting today, you can connect your helpdesk directly to Slack and automatically deliver a snapshot of your open tickets to any channel you choose, at the exact time your team starts their day.

---

### What’s New?

With this integration, you can automate cross-functional visibility without burning extra helpdesk licenses:

* **A Clear Daily Snapshot:** Receive a clean, scannable breakdown of open tickets categorized by priority, status, and aging. 
* **Custom Channel Routing:** Choose exactly where the update lands. Send a high-priority alert to your `#tier-3-engineering` channel, a general digest to `#support-team`, or a birds-eye view to `#leadership`.
* **Zero Context-Switching:** Your engineering, product, and leadership teams can monitor customer health and jump on blockers directly from where they already work.
* **Direct Deep Links:** Every summary links directly back to the relevant views in your helpdesk, making triage just a click away.

---

### How to Get Started in 3 Steps

Setting up your daily summary takes less than two minutes:

1. **Go to Settings:** Navigate to **Settings > Integrations** in your dashboard and select **Slack**.
2. **Authenticate & Choose Your Channel:** Click **Connect**, authorize the app, and pick the public or private Slack channel where you want updates delivered.
3. **Set Your Schedule:** Choose the time and days you want the digest posted (e.g., weekdays at 9:00 AM).

Hit **Save**, and your next summary will arrive on schedule!
