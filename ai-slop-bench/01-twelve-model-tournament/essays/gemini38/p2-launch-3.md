### Your queue at a glance, right where you work

With our new Slack integration, [Product Name] can automatically post a clear, actionable summary of all open support tickets directly to the public or private Slack channel of your choice, every single morning.

No extra logins. No digging through dashboards. Just the operational visibility your team needs to stay aligned, resolve issues faster, and prevent SLA breaches.

### What’s included in the digest?

*   **Total Open Ticket Count:** Instantly see the size of your active backlog.
*   **Priority Breakdown:** Know immediately how many Urgent, High, and Normal priority tickets are waiting.
*   **SLA Warnings:** Spot tickets at risk of breaching response or resolution targets before it happens.
*   **Direct Links:** Jump directly from the Slack message into any specific ticket with a single click.

### Complete control over when and where it posts

You decide how this fits your workflow. Choose a team-wide channel like `#support-standup` to kick off the day, or send it to an executive channel like `#leadership-updates` for high-level visibility. You can customize the delivery time to match your team’s morning standup or shift handoff across time zones.

### How to set it up in 2 minutes:

1. Head to **Settings > Integrations** in your [Product Name] dashboard.
2. Select **Slack** and click **Connect**.
3. Choose your preferred channel and set your daily delivery time.
4. Click **Save**—your first digest will arrive at your scheduled time!
