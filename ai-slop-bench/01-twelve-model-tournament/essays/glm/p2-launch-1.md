**Subject:** Introducing Daily Ticket Digest: your support queue, now in Slack

Hi [First Name],

If your team is anything like the ones we talked to while building this feature, your day starts in Slack — but your ticket queue lives somewhere else. Keeping everyone aligned has meant tab-switching, exporting reports, or asking someone to paste numbers into a channel every morning.

Starting today, that's automated. **Daily Ticket Digest** posts a summary of your open support tickets to any Slack channel you choose, at the time you choose.

**What's in each digest**

- Total open tickets, with the change since yesterday
- New tickets opened in the last 24 hours
- Tickets approaching or breaching SLA
- Oldest unresolved tickets
- Unassigned tickets that need an owner

Every line links straight to a filtered view in [Product], so anyone on the team can click through and pick up work — no more "what's the status?"

**Why we built it**

We heard from teams whose standups began with someone reading numbers off a dashboard. The information already existed; it just wasn't where the team was. The digest puts a shared, up-to-date picture of the queue in front of everyone, every morning — no dashboard required.

**Set it up in two minutes**

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations → Slack** and connect your workspace (your Slack admin may need to approve the first time).
2. Choose the channel where you'd like the digest posted — #support-ops is a popular pick.
3. Pick a delivery time. Most teams go with 9:00 a.m., right before standup.

That's it. Your first digest arrives tomorrow at your chosen time.

**A few things worth knowing**

- Available on all plans, at no extra cost.
- The digest contains counts and ticket links only — no customer message content is ever posted to Slack.
- Admins can set up digests for multiple channels, so each team or queue can get its own summary.
