**Subject: Now live — your daily open-ticket summary, delivered right to Slack**

Hi there,

Quick question: how many times a day does someone on your team open the helpdesk dashboard just to check how many tickets are open? If the answer is "too many," this one's for you.

We're excited to announce our new Slack integration: a daily summary of your open support tickets, posted automatically to the Slack channel of your choice.

**What you'll get**

Every day, at a time you choose, we'll post a clean, digestible summary straight to your team's channel:

- **Total open tickets**, with day-over-day change
- **New tickets** opened in the last 24 hours
- **At-risk tickets** approaching or breaching SLA
- **Aging tickets** — your oldest unresolved issues, so nothing slips through the cracks
- **A breakdown by priority, queue, or assignee**

Each ticket links directly back to the helpdesk, so anyone who needs to dig in can get there in one click.

**Why we built it**

Support doesn't happen in a vacuum. Managers want a daily pulse check without running reports. Colleagues in other departments want to know what's coming without asking. And your team leads want at-risk tickets surfaced *before* they become escalations — not after.

Now everyone can start the day with the same picture of what's open, what's urgent, and what needs attention — without anyone leaving Slack.

**Getting started takes about two minutes**

1. In your helpdesk, go to **Settings → Integrations → Slack** and connect your workspace (no new logins required).
2. Choose the channel where you'd like the summary posted — #support-ops, #leadership, wherever it's most useful.
3. Pick your posting time. Most teams choose first thing in the morning.
4. Save. Your first digest arrives tomorrow.

**A few things worth knowing**

- The integration is available on **all plans at no additional cost**.
- Summaries only post to channels your Slack admin permits, and only channel members can see them — no ticket data is shared beyond what you choose.
- You can change your channel, timing, or digest contents anytime in Settings.
