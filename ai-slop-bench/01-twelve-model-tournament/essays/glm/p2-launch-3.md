Your team doesn't live in the helpdesk — it lives in Slack. That's why we're excited to announce **Daily Digest for Slack**, available to all [Product] customers starting today.

**What it does**

Every morning at a time you choose, Daily Digest posts a summary of your open support tickets directly to any Slack channel you pick. No dashboards to open, no reports to run — just a clean, scannable snapshot of where things stand, delivered where your team already works.

Each digest includes:

- **Total open tickets**, plus how many came in since yesterday's digest
- **Unassigned tickets**, so nothing slips through the cracks
- **Tickets approaching or breaching SLA**, flagged so you can act before it's too late
- **Your oldest open tickets**, so long-running issues stay on the radar

**Why we built it**

The most common question in any support team's Slack is some version of "what's the status on tickets?" Daily Digest answers it before anyone has to ask. Managers get an at-a-glance view of workload and risk. Agents start the day knowing exactly what needs attention first. And everyone else — product, engineering, leadership — gets visibility without requesting a report or logging in.

One beta customer told us their morning standup went from twenty minutes of status-hunting to five minutes of problem-solving. That's exactly what we were hoping for.

**Setup takes about two minutes**

1. In [Product], go to **Settings → Integrations → Slack** and connect your workspace (if you haven't already).
2. Choose the channel where you'd like the digest posted.
3. Pick your delivery time — most teams choose just before standup.

That's it. Tomorrow morning, your first digest lands in Slack. You can change the channel, time, or contents at any point, and you can set up separate digests for different teams or queues — one for Tier 1, one for escalations, whatever fits how you work.
