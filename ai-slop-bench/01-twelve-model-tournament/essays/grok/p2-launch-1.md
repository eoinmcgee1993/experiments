**Stay on top of open tickets—without leaving Slack**

We’re rolling out a new Slack integration for existing customers: a daily summary of your open support tickets, posted automatically to a channel you choose.

If your team already lives in Slack, this is a simple way to keep ticket volume, aging work, and priorities visible without extra logins or status meetings.

**What you’ll get each day**

Once enabled, the integration posts a concise digest of open tickets to the Slack channel you select. The summary is designed for a quick scan so agents, leads, and stakeholders can see what’s still outstanding before the day gets busy.

Typical highlights include:

- Total open tickets  
- Breakdown by priority or status (where your workspace uses them)  
- Tickets that have been waiting longest  
- A clear snapshot so nothing sits unnoticed in the queue  

You pick the channel—#support, #customer-success, a private ops channel, or whatever already matches how your team works.

**Why it matters**

Helpdesk dashboards are still the source of truth. Daily Slack summaries are the reminder layer: they reduce context-switching, surface backlog earlier, and give the whole team a shared view of open work. Leaders can spot volume spikes; agents can jump on aging tickets; nobody has to remember to check the inbox first thing.

It’s especially useful for distributed teams and anyone who already starts the morning in Slack.

**How to turn it on**

In your helpdesk settings, open Integrations, connect Slack, authorize the workspace, and choose the destination channel. Set the daily post time that fits your team (for example, start of business in your primary timezone). You can change the channel or disable the digest at any time.

Setup takes a few minutes. No change to how tickets are created, assigned, or resolved—only an extra, optional visibility channel.

**Get started**

The Slack daily summary is available now for existing customers. Enable it from Integrations in your account, or reach out to your customer success contact if you want a walkthrough.

We built this because you asked for less dashboard-hopping and more signal where you already collaborate. We hope it makes open tickets easier to see—and easier to close.
