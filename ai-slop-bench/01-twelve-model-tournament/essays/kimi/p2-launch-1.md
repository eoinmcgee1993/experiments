# New: Daily Ticket Summaries, Delivered to Slack

*Start every day knowing exactly what's in your support queue — without opening a new tab.*

Your team's support workload shouldn't live behind a login. Today, we're launching our new Slack integration: an automatic daily summary of your open tickets, posted to any Slack channel you choose.

## Why we built it

You told us visibility was the gap. Support agents live in the queue, but managers, engineers, and the rest of the company live in Slack — and they had no easy way to see what customers were asking about. Checking in meant another login, another tab, another interruption.

The daily summary closes that gap. Every morning, a clear snapshot of your open tickets arrives where your team already works, so everyone starts the day on the same page.

## What's in each summary

Posted at the time you choose, each digest includes:

- **Total open tickets**, broken down by priority
- **Unassigned tickets** that still need an owner
- **Tickets awaiting a response**, so nothing goes stale
- **Your oldest open ticket**, because aging issues shouldn't hide
- **A direct link** to jump straight into the full queue

One message. Ten seconds to read. A complete picture of the day ahead.

## Set up in under two minutes

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations → Slack**
2. Click **Connect to Slack** and authorize your workspace
3. Pick your channel and delivery time
4. Save — your first summary arrives tomorrow morning

No admin approval workflows, no configuration files, no downtime.

## A few ways teams are using it

- **Support leads** post summaries to their team channel each morning to kick off triage
- **Engineering teams** follow the support channel to spot bug reports trending upward
- **Executives** get visibility into support health without asking for a report
