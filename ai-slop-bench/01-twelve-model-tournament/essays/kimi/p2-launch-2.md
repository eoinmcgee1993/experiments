# New: Your Daily Ticket Digest, Delivered Straight to Slack

**Start every day with a clear picture of your support queue — no dashboard required.**

You shouldn't have to log in to your helpdesk just to find out what's happening in it. So today, we're launching a new addition to our Slack integration: a **daily summary of open support tickets**, posted automatically to the Slack channel of your choice.

## How it works

Each morning, at the time you choose, we'll post a snapshot of your queue to any channel in your Slack workspace — #support, #team-leads, or wherever it's most useful. Every summary includes:

- **Total open tickets**, broken down by priority and status
- **New tickets** from the last 24 hours
- **At-risk tickets** approaching their SLA deadlines
- **Unassigned tickets** that still need an owner
- A direct link to jump into the full queue

## Why you'll love it

**Fewer surprises.** Overdue and SLA-risk tickets surface before they turn into escalations, so you can act while there's still time.

**Better standups.** Walk into your morning meeting already knowing the shape of the queue. No more opening five tabs to prep.

**Less context switching.** Team leads, managers, and execs get visibility into support workload without needing another login — or interrupting your agents to ask.

## Make it yours

This isn't a one-size-fits-all blast. You control:

- **Which channel** receives the summary
- **What time** it posts, in your timezone
- **What's included** — turn sections on or off
- **Which tickets count** — filter by team, inbox, or priority, so your escalation channel only sees escalation tickets

Set up multiple summaries if you like: a high-level digest for leadership, a detailed one for your frontline team.

## Get set up in two minutes

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations → Slack**
2. Connect your Slack workspace (or reconnect if you've used our Slack app before)
3. Pick your channel, set your schedule, and choose your filters

That's it. Your first summary arrives tomorrow morning.
