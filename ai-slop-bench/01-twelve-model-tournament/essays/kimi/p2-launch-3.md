# Start every morning on top of your support queue

**Introducing daily ticket summaries in Slack** 🎉

You asked, we listened. Our new Slack integration posts a daily summary of your open support tickets straight to the channel of your choice — so your whole team starts the day knowing exactly where things stand, without anyone logging in to pull a report.

## Why we built it

Support work happens in the helpdesk, but your team lives in Slack. Managers told us they were manually sharing ticket counts every morning, and agents said aging tickets sometimes went unnoticed until a customer followed up. The daily summary closes that gap: one automatic message, every morning, with everything you need to triage the day ahead.

## What's in each summary

Every post gives you a clear snapshot of your open queue:

- **Total open tickets** — and how that compares to yesterday
- **Breakdown by priority**, so urgent issues stand out
- **Unassigned tickets** that still need an owner
- **Your oldest open tickets**, so nothing quietly goes stale
- **A direct link** to jump into the full queue

## Set up in under a minute

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations → Slack**
2. Click **Connect to Slack** and authorize your workspace
3. Choose your channel — a team channel like #support, a leadership channel, or a private channel for your on-call rotation
4. Save. Your first summary arrives tomorrow morning.

You can change the channel or disconnect at any time from the same page.

## Good to know

- Summaries post at 8:00 AM in your account's timezone (configurable in settings)
- Works with both public and private channels
- Only aggregate counts and ticket metadata are shared — no customer message content ever appears in Slack
- Available today on **all plans at no extra cost**
