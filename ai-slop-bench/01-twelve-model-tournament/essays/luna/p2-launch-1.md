# Stay on top of support with our new Slack integration

We’re excited to introduce a new way to keep your team aligned on customer support: **daily open-ticket summaries in Slack**.

With the new Slack integration, your helpdesk can post a summary of open support tickets to a channel of your choice—so your team can quickly see what needs attention without leaving Slack or searching through the helpdesk.

Each daily summary gives your team a clear view of outstanding work, helping you:

- Start the day with a shared understanding of support priorities
- Keep open customer issues visible to the right people
- Reduce the need for manual status updates
- Coordinate more easily across support, engineering, product, and operations

## Choose where updates appear

Send your daily summary to the Slack channel that works best for your team. Use an existing support channel, create a dedicated ticket-triage channel, or share updates with a broader cross-functional group.

## Get started

Connect Slack from your helpdesk settings, select the channel where you want summaries posted, and keep your team informed automatically each day.

The integration is available now for existing customers. Set it up today and make open-ticket visibility a natural part of your team’s daily workflow.
