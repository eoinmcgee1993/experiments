# Stay on top of support with our new Slack integration

We’re excited to introduce a simpler way to keep your team informed about customer support: **daily open-ticket summaries in Slack**.

With the new Slack integration, your helpdesk can post a summary of open support tickets to the channel of your choice—so everyone stays aligned without switching between tools or searching through inboxes.

## What you’ll get

Each daily summary gives your team a quick view of outstanding support work, helping you:

- See how many tickets are still open
- Keep customer issues visible to the right team
- Spot follow-ups that may need attention
- Start the day with a shared view of support priorities

Choose the Slack channel that makes the most sense for your team—whether that’s a dedicated support channel, a customer-success workspace, or a team-wide updates channel.

## Get started

Connect Slack from your helpdesk settings, select the channel where you’d like summaries posted, and you’re ready to go.

The integration is available now for existing customers. Set it up today and make support visibility part of your team’s daily workflow.
