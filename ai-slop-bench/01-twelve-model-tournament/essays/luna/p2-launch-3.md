# Stay on top of support with daily ticket summaries in Slack

We’re excited to introduce a new Slack integration that makes it easier for your team to keep track of open support requests—without leaving the tools you already use every day.

With the new integration, your helpdesk can post a daily summary of open tickets to any Slack channel you choose. Get a clear snapshot of what needs attention, keep support visible across your organization, and help your team stay aligned without manually pulling reports or checking multiple systems.

## What’s included

Each daily summary can help your team quickly understand:

- How many support tickets are currently open
- Which tickets need attention
- Ticket priorities and statuses
- Key details and links back to your helpdesk

Choose the Slack channel that makes the most sense for your workflow—whether that’s a dedicated support channel, an operations channel, or a team channel shared with other stakeholders.

## Keep support moving

Daily visibility helps your team spot bottlenecks, follow up on urgent issues, and coordinate more effectively. It also gives non-support teams an easy way to stay informed about customer needs without requiring access to your helpdesk.

The integration is designed to fit into your existing process: connect Slack, select a channel, and let the daily updates come to you.

## Get started

To set up the Slack integration:

1. Open your helpdesk settings.
2. Navigate to **Integrations** and select **Slack**.
3. Connect your Slack workspace.
4. Choose the channel where you’d like to receive daily summaries.
5. Save your settings.

Once enabled, your team will receive a daily summary of open support tickets in the selected channel.

We hope this makes it easier to stay informed, prioritize work, and deliver great support—right from Slack.
