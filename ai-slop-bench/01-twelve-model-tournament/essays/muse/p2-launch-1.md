**New Feature: Daily Ticket Summaries for Slack**

Hi there,

Starting your day buried in the support queue? Not anymore.

We're excited to launch **Daily Digest for Slack** — a simple new integration that posts a clear, skimmable summary of your open support tickets to a Slack channel of your choice, every morning.

Built for busy support teams, it's the easiest way to keep everyone aligned without living in the helpdesk.

**What you get**

Every weekday morning at the time you choose, you'll get an automated post with:

* Total open tickets, broken down by status and priority
* New tickets since yesterday
* Tickets at risk of breaching SLA
* Your 5 oldest unresolved tickets, with direct links

No digging. No manual reports. Just the context you need to prioritize, right where your team already works.

**Why you'll love it**

Customers in our beta told us it changed their standups. Support leads spot bottlenecks faster, agents know what to tackle first, and stakeholders outside support — like product and success — finally have visibility without needing another login.

**How to turn it on**

It takes less than 2 minutes:

1. Go to **Settings > Integrations > Slack** and click Connect
2. Choose the channel where you want the digest (we recommend #support-daily)
3. Pick your delivery time and timezone

That's it. Your first digest will arrive tomorrow morning.
