**Never Miss an Open Ticket Again: Daily Summaries in Slack Are Here**

Hi there,

We have news you'll see first thing tomorrow morning — literally.

Starting today, our helpdesk connects with Slack to post a daily summary of your open support tickets right to the channel of your choice. No more tab-switching, digging through dashboards, or asking "what's still open?" in standup.

We built this for busy support teams who live in Slack. Now everyone — from agents to managers to engineering partners — starts the day with the same clear picture.

Here's what your Daily Digest includes:

* Total open tickets, grouped by status and priority
* Tickets waiting longest and those breaching SLA soon
* New tickets since yesterday and recently resolved
* Direct links to jump into any ticket in one click

You choose the channel (#support-daily, #cs-escalations, you name it) and the time it posts, so it fits how your team already works — whether that's a 9 a.m. triage ritual or an async check-in across time zones.

Getting started takes less than 2 minutes:

1. Go to Settings > Integrations > Slack in your helpdesk admin.
2. Connect your Slack workspace and pick a channel.
3. Choose your delivery time and timezone, then hit Save.

That's it. Your first summary will arrive on the next scheduled run.

This integration is live now for all existing customers at no additional cost. Admin permission is required to connect, but once it's on, the whole channel can benefit.
