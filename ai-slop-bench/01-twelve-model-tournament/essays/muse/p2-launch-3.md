**Introducing: Daily Ticket Digest for Slack 🎉**

Hi there,

You asked, we built it. We're thrilled to announce a new way to keep your whole team aligned on support — without ever leaving Slack.

Starting today, you can get an automatic **daily summary of your open support tickets** posted right to a Slack channel of your choice.

No more tab-switching. No more morning status hunts. Just one clear snapshot to start your day.

### What you get

Every morning at a time you choose, our bot will post to e.g. #support-daily with:

* **Open tickets at-a-glance:** Total count broken down by status, priority, and assignee
* **Needs attention:** Unassigned, unanswered, and oldest waiting tickets
* **SLA watchlist:** Tickets approaching or breaching SLA so nothing slips
* **One-click actions:** Direct links to jump into any ticket in the helpdesk

It's perfect for morning standups, triage, and keeping non-support stakeholders like engineering and success in the loop.

### Why you'll love it

1.  **Total visibility:** Everyone sees the same queue.
2.  **Faster prioritization:** Spot bottlenecks before your customers feel them.
3.  **Less noise:** One helpful digest, not a ping for every update.

### Get started in 2 minutes

1. Go to **Settings > Integrations > Slack** and click Connect
2. Authorize with Slack and pick a channel
3. Choose your delivery time, timezone, and filters (by team, brand, or priority)

And you're done. Your first digest will arrive tomorrow morning.

The Slack Daily Digest is available now at no extra cost for all customers on Pro plans and above, using secure OAuth.

Try it this week and let us know what you think — we'd love your feedback as we add weekly trends and customizable digests next.
