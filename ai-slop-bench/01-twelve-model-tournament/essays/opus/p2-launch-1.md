# Your ticket queue, now in Slack: introducing Daily Ticket Digest

Starting today, you can connect [Product] to Slack and have a summary of your open support tickets posted automatically to any channel you choose.

## The problem we heard from you

Support data has a habit of staying inside the support tool. Your agents live in the queue all day, but everyone else — engineering leads, account managers, your COO — only finds out about a backlog or an aging escalation when someone thinks to mention it.

Many of you told us you'd built workarounds: a manual morning post in #support, a scheduled report nobody opens, a weekly meeting that's mostly reading numbers aloud. Daily Ticket Digest replaces all of that with a single automated message.

## What the digest includes

Each digest gives your team a snapshot of the queue at a glance:

- **Total open tickets**, with the change since yesterday
- **Tickets breaching or approaching SLA**, listed individually with links
- **Oldest unresolved tickets**, so nothing quietly ages out of view
- **Breakdown by priority and by assignee**
- **New tickets created and tickets resolved** in the last 24 hours

Every ticket reference links straight back to [Product], so following up is one click, not a search.

## Configurable to your workflow

You control what the digest looks like and who sees it:

- **Choose the channel.** Public, private, or a DM to yourself — post wherever the conversation already happens.
- **Set the schedule.** Pick the delivery time and time zone. Weekdays only is an option if you'd rather not ping the channel on Saturday.
- **Filter the scope.** Send a digest for a specific team, product area, priority level, or customer segment. Many teams run two: a full queue digest to #support and a high-priority-only digest to #leadership.
- **Run multiple digests.** No limit on how many you configure.

## Setting it up

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations → Slack**
2. Click **Connect to Slack** and authorize the app in your workspace
3. Click **New digest**, choose your channel, schedule, and filters
4. Use **Send test digest** to preview it immediately

Setup takes about two minutes. You'll need admin permissions in [Product] and the ability to add apps to your Slack workspace — if your Slack is locked down, your workspace admin may need to approve the app first.

## Availability and pricing

Daily Ticket Digest is included at no additional cost on all current plans, and it's live in your account now. It's read-only by design: the integration posts to Slack and never modifies your ticket data.
