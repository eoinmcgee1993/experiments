**Now available: Daily ticket summaries in Slack**

Keep your whole team on top of open support tickets — without anyone logging in.

---

Starting today, you can connect [Product] to Slack and have a summary of your open support tickets posted automatically to any channel you choose.

**Why we built it**

Support context tends to stay locked inside the helpdesk. Managers ping agents for status updates, engineers miss the bug report that's been escalated three times this week, and account owners hear about an unhappy customer only after the renewal call. Meanwhile, everyone already lives in Slack.

The daily summary puts the state of your queue where your team is already looking, so the right people notice the right tickets — before they turn into problems.

**What's in the summary**

Each post includes:

- **Total open tickets**, with the change since yesterday
- **Tickets breaching or approaching SLA**, listed individually
- **Oldest unanswered tickets**, so nothing quietly ages out
- **New tickets opened** in the last 24 hours
- **Tickets closed** in the last 24 hours
- A breakdown by **assignee**, **priority**, or **tag** — your choice

Every ticket in the summary links straight back to [Product], so anyone with a seat can click through and reply in one step.

**Set it up in about two minutes**

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations → Slack** and click **Connect**.
2. Authorize the [Product] app in your Slack workspace and pick a destination channel (public or private).
3. Choose your delivery time, time zone, and which sections to include. Save.

That's it. Your first summary arrives at the next scheduled time. You can send a test post immediately if you'd rather not wait.

**A few things worth knowing**

- **Multiple channels are supported.** Send an all-queues summary to #support and a filtered view to #engineering or a specific customer channel. Each connection has its own filters and schedule.
- **You control what's shared.** Summaries can be limited by view, tag, priority, or group, and you can choose whether ticket subjects are included or replaced with ticket IDs only — useful for channels with guests or contractors.
- **Permissions are respected.** Clicking a ticket link takes users to [Product], where your existing access rules apply. Posting to Slack doesn't grant anyone new visibility inside the helpdesk.
- **Weekends off.** Skip Saturdays and Sundays, or any days you like, with one toggle.

**Availability and pricing**

The Slack integration is included at no extra cost on [Plan] and above, and is available now in all regions. Workspace admin approval may be required to install the app — if you hit that wall, your Slack admin can approve it from the Slack app directory.
