**Subject: New Feature: Get a Daily Open Ticket Summary in Slack**

Hi [First Name],

We’re excited to share a new way to keep support visibility top of mind without leaving Slack: the **Daily Support Ticket Summary** integration.

Starting today, [Product Name] can post a concise daily update to a Slack channel of your choice, summarizing your open support tickets. The goal is simple: help teams, account managers, and support leaders stay aligned on what’s open, what’s urgent, and what’s at risk—without digging through dashboards or switching contexts first thing in the morning.

**What’s included in the daily summary?**

The message can include:

- Total number of open tickets
- Tickets with high priority or at-risk SLAs
- Tickets awaiting customer response
- Tickets assigned to each support team or group
- A link back to your dashboard for deeper exploration

You decide which Slack channel receives the summary—whether it’s a private support channel, a shared account management channel, or a leadership update channel.

**Why we built it**

We heard from many of you that the biggest challenge isn’t always tracking each individual ticket—it’s maintaining a shared understanding of support workload and customer risk across the organization. A daily Slack summary makes it easier for teams to notice trends, start conversations early, and follow up on high-priority items before they become escalations.

**How to get started**

1. Go to **Settings > Integrations** in [Product Name]
2. Select **Slack**
3. Choose the channel where you’d like daily summaries posted
4. Customize the time and ticket filters, if needed
5. Save your settings

Once enabled, you’ll receive your first daily summary on the next scheduled delivery.
