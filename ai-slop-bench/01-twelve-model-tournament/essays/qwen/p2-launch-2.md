**New Feature Launch: Daily Open Ticket Summaries in Slack**

We’re introducing a new Slack integration that keeps your team aligned with a daily summary of open support tickets—delivered directly to a Slack channel of your choice.

Now available for existing customers, this feature makes it easier to see what’s on your support team’s plate without logging into the helpdesk. Each day, you’ll receive a clear, concise update highlighting open tickets so managers, agents, and cross-functional stakeholders can prioritize work, spot trends, and stay proactive.

Instead of chasing status updates or digging through queues, your team can start each day with the same shared view of support activity. Whether you use Slack for daily standups, team updates, or operational reviews, these summaries help keep everyone focused on the customer conversations that need attention next.

**What you’ll see in your daily summary**

- Total open support tickets  
- Tickets that may need priority attention  
- A quick overview of workload across your team or queue  
- A simple way to stay up to date without switching tools

**Why it matters**

Support teams often manage competing priorities across email, chat, phone, and escalations. This integration reduces manual checking and keeps critical ticket information visible to the right people at the right time. It supports faster triage, better collaboration, and stronger daily planning—especially for teams managing high ticket volumes or shared accountability across departments.

**How to get started**

Connecting the integration is simple. Choose the Slack channel where you want the daily summary to appear, select the ticket queue or view you want to track, and enable the update. From there, the summary will be posted automatically each day.

If you’d like help setting it up, our support team can guide you through the steps.

With this launch, we’re making it easier for your team to stay informed, organized, and ready to support your customers—without leaving Slack.
