# Stay on top of open tickets with daily Slack summaries

We’re excited to introduce our new Slack integration, designed to help your team keep support work visible and moving.

Once enabled, the integration posts a daily summary of open support tickets to the Slack channel of your choice. Your team gets a clear, consistent view of outstanding requests—without needing to leave Slack or manually compile updates.

With daily ticket summaries, you can:

- Keep open support issues visible across the team  
- Quickly identify tickets that need attention  
- Make daily stand-ups and handoffs more efficient  
- Give stakeholders better visibility into support workloads  
- Reduce time spent checking dashboards or sharing status updates  

Choose the Slack channel that works best for your workflow, whether it’s a dedicated support channel, an operations channel, or a shared space for customer-facing teams.

Ready to get started? Open your integration settings, connect Slack, select a channel, and enable daily summaries.

Turn open tickets into shared priorities—right where your team already works.
