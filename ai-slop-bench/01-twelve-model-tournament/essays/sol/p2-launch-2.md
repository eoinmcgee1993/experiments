# Stay on top of open tickets with daily Slack summaries

We’re excited to introduce our new Slack integration, designed to keep your team aligned on support work without adding another dashboard to check.

Once connected, the integration posts a daily summary of open support tickets to the Slack channel of your choice. Your team gets a clear, consistent view of what needs attention—right where they already collaborate.

With daily ticket summaries, you can:

- Keep open support requests visible across the team
- Quickly identify outstanding work and follow-up priorities
- Give stakeholders an easy way to stay informed
- Reduce time spent manually sharing ticket updates

Choose the Slack channel that works best for your workflow, whether it’s a dedicated support channel, a team operations channel, or a shared space for customer updates.

Ready to get started? Open your integration settings, connect your Slack workspace, and select the channel where you’d like the daily summary to appear.

Connect Slack today and help your team start every day with a shared view of open support tickets.
