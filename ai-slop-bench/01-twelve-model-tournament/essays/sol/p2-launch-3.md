# Stay on top of open tickets with daily Slack summaries

Your support team can now get a daily summary of open tickets delivered directly to Slack.

Our new Slack integration makes it easier to keep outstanding customer requests visible—without requiring your team to check the helpdesk throughout the day. Choose the Slack channel that works best for your team, and a summary of open support tickets will be posted there each day.

With daily Slack summaries, you can:

- Keep open tickets top of mind
- Give support teams and stakeholders shared visibility
- Make daily triage and handoffs easier
- Reduce the need to switch between tools

Whether you send the summary to a dedicated support channel or a broader customer operations channel, your team can quickly see what still needs attention and coordinate next steps in Slack.

To get started, open your integration settings, connect your Slack workspace, and choose the channel where you’d like the daily summary to appear.

Connect Slack today and help your team stay aligned on every open ticket.
