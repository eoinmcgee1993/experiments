## New: Daily Open Ticket Summaries in Slack

Keeping up with support shouldn’t require another dashboard check.

We’re excited to introduce our new **Slack integration**, which delivers a daily summary of your open support tickets directly to the Slack channel of your choice.

Each day, your team can receive a clear snapshot of outstanding support work—making it easier to stay aligned on customer issues, spot priorities, and take action without leaving Slack.

### What’s included

Your daily Slack summary gives your team visibility into open tickets at a glance, including:

- Total number of open tickets
- Tickets grouped by status or priority
- Key ticket details to help identify what needs attention
- Direct links back to your helpdesk for quick follow-up

Whether you share it in a dedicated support channel, an account-management channel, or a broader operations workspace, the summary helps the right people stay informed.

### Why it matters

Support is a team effort. With daily ticket summaries in Slack, teams can:

- Keep customer-facing teams aware of unresolved issues
- Surface urgent or aging tickets before they slip through the cracks
- Reduce manual status updates and dashboard checking
- Create a shared daily rhythm around support priorities

### Getting started

An administrator can connect your Slack workspace and choose the channel where daily summaries should be posted. Once configured, your team will begin receiving scheduled updates automatically.

Visit **Settings → Integrations → Slack** to connect your workspace and set up your daily summary.

We’re excited to help your team keep support work visible, coordinated, and moving forward.
