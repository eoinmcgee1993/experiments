## New: Daily Open Ticket Summaries in Slack

Keeping your support team aligned just got easier.

You can now connect your helpdesk to Slack and receive a daily summary of open support tickets in the channel of your choice. Give your team a clear view of outstanding work—without needing to log in and check dashboards first thing in the morning.

Each daily summary helps your team quickly understand what needs attention, including the current volume of open tickets and the tickets that may require follow-up.

### Why it matters

With daily ticket visibility in Slack, your team can:

- Start the day with a shared view of open support work  
- Keep support, customer success, and account teams in sync  
- Spot growing backlogs before they become urgent  
- Discuss priorities and coordinate next steps where your team already works  

Whether you send the summary to a dedicated support channel or a cross-functional team channel, everyone who needs visibility can stay informed.

### Get started

Set up the Slack integration from your helpdesk settings, choose the Slack channel where you’d like summaries delivered, and enable daily notifications.

Once connected, your team will receive a reliable daily snapshot of open tickets—right in Slack.

Connect Slack today and make sure no open customer conversation gets overlooked.
