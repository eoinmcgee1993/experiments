# New: Get Your Daily Support Ticket Summary in Slack

Keeping up with open support requests just got easier.

We’re excited to introduce our new **Slack integration**, which automatically posts a daily summary of your open support tickets to the Slack channel you choose.

Whether your support team works primarily in Slack or you want stakeholders to stay informed without logging into the helpdesk, the daily summary gives everyone a clear view of what needs attention.

## Stay aligned on open tickets

Each daily Slack update provides a quick snapshot of your current support workload, helping your team:

- Monitor open ticket volume at a glance  
- Spot requests that may need follow-up  
- Keep support, customer success, and operations teams aligned  
- Start the day with a shared understanding of priorities  

Send the summary to a dedicated support channel, an internal team channel, or any other Slack destination that fits your workflow.

## Simple setup, useful visibility

To get started, connect your Slack workspace and select the channel where you’d like to receive updates. Once enabled, your daily open-ticket summary will be delivered automatically—no manual reporting required.

This integration is designed to make support visibility easier while keeping your team in the tools they already use every day.

Ready to enable it? Head to your **Integrations** settings to connect Slack and choose your daily summary channel.
